Window tint guides & FAQs

How do you prepare the vehicle for tinting?

We inspect the glass and nearby trim before installation. If your vehicle has delicate trim or electronics near the glass, let us know so we can review the setup with you.

Does darker tint reject more heat?

Darkness alone doesn’t tell you how a film handles heat. Compare performance at the shades you’re considering. IR rejection measures infrared energy, while TSER measures total solar energy rejected.

What’s the difference between carbon and ceramic?

Carbon is our starting option for appearance, privacy, and glare reduction. Ceramic IR and Ceramic Plus are upgrades for drivers who prioritize infrared heat rejection.

Can I replace bubbling or discolored tint?

Yes. Share which windows already have film. We can quote removal and replacement based on the vehicle and the condition of the existing film.

What tint is legal in Texas? Can I cover my whole windshield?

Texas DPS says the front side windows must transmit at least 25% of visible light through the glass and film combined. DPS allows clear, untinted UV film across the front windshield. Tinted windshield strips are subject to placement and other limits.
